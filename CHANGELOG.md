# Changelog

## 1.5.0
- Tracker: click the current stop to read its quest in the quest log; right-click an upcoming stop does the same. Ctrl-click works on the panel's Next and Route tabs.
- Hard quests last: elite, group, dungeon and 3+ levels-above-you quests go after every easier quest on the route and the Next tab, and are labelled.
- The route is untangled after it's planned (no doubling back), and starting the guide re-plans from where you stand.
- Where to level: zones holding your quests, quests seen offered there, and unexplored ground rank higher, with the reasons shown; other continents are suggested when nothing on yours fits.
- Discovery announcements for new areas and each quarter of a zone explored.
- `/sol stats` and the tracker header: XP per hour, time to level, quests and discoveries this session.
- Version awareness: after an update the first login lists what's new (also `/sol version` and Options → About), and you're told when a guild or group mate runs a newer version.

## 1.4.0
- New look for the route tracker and options window: one expedition theme with a bronze frame and gold accents.
- Options are now organised in a sidebar of categories, each setting explained under its name.
- Tracker: the current stop is a card with a direction arrow and live distance, a progress strip shows how far along the route you are, and the background opacity is adjustable.

## 1.3.0
- The route now follows objective progress: picking up one of several items moves the guide to the next camp automatically.
- Kill and collect objectives are search areas; the tracker says "in the area" once you're inside the radius (adjustable).
- `/sol pin` can record several objective spots (`add`), the hand-in (`turnin`) or forget them (`clear`), and rebuilds the route.

## 1.2.0
- Route by quest level, mark high-level quests, leave out dungeon quests.
- Keep the route current as you accept, finish and hand in quests; fixed duplicate stops; diagnostics saved for bug reports.
