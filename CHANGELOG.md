# Changelog

## 1.4.0
- New look for the route tracker and options window: one expedition theme with a bronze frame and gold accents.
- Options are now organised in a sidebar of categories, each setting explained under its name.
- Tracker: the current stop is a card with a direction arrow and live distance, a progress strip shows how far along the route you are, and the background opacity is adjustable.

## 1.3.0
- The route now follows objective progress: picking up one of several items moves the guide to the next camp automatically.
- Kill and collect objectives are search areas; the tracker says "in the area" once you're inside the radius (adjustable).
- `/sol pin` can record several objective spots (`add`), the hand-in (`turnin`) or forget them (`clear`), and rebuilds the route.

## 1.2.0
- Route by quest level, mark high-level quests, leave out dungeon quests.
- Keep the route current as you accept, finish and hand in quests; fixed duplicate stops; diagnostics saved for bug reports.
